Summary

Start and recent are the best estimates in a variation’s first and last 5 weeks. kg / month is a straight-line fit through every session’s best.

Estimated 1RM uses the Epley formula: weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30). A single counts as its own weight. — sets of more than 10 reps are left out, because rep-based estimates are unreliable that high. That leaves — of — logged squat, bench and deadlift sets. Variations with fewer than 3 sessions appear only in the summary table. The three tiles at the top show the competition variations: Squat · Lowbar, Bench · Comp. Bench and Deadlift · Conventional. Sets up to 9 Feb 2025 come from Summary Training, later ones from the training plan, with variation names as each source writes them.

Accessories

One chart per exercise. Each dot is one session’s best set by estimated 1RM (Epley, weight × (1 + reps / 30), applied to all rep counts), so the same weight for more reps ranks higher; the line connects the sessions, hover for the set. With 15+ reps the estimate is rough, so read it as a trend rather than a real max. Leg extension values are machine settings, not kg. Sets without added weight are left out, and so are the seated leg curl sets over 20 kg, which were on another machine.
